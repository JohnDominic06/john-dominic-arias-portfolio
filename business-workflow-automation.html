CASE STUDY

The problem

Many administrative workflows involve repeatedly copying information, updating spreadsheets, checking records, and communicating status updates. When these tasks are performed manually, they can consume time and make information harder to track.

The approach

A centralized spreadsheet can act as the starting point for a structured workflow. Information can then be validated, organized, and processed through simple automation.

Before Automation

  • Manually review incoming information
  • Repeated data entry
  • Manually update status fields
  • Search through multiple records
  • Manually prepare routine reports

With Workflow Automation

  • Centralized information
  • Consistent data structure
  • Automated routine updates
  • Easier status tracking
  • Faster reporting workflows
