OVERVIEW

Keeping e-commerce operations organized.

E-commerce operations often involve multiple moving parts: customer orders, inventory information, fulfillment, shipping details, and operational records.

This portfolio demonstration shows how I would structure those activities into a clear workflow that helps reduce confusion, improve visibility, and keep orders moving through the fulfillment process.

THE CHALLENGE

Multiple systems can create operational complexity.

01

Order Management

Orders need to be reviewed, processed, and monitored throughout the fulfillment workflow.

02

Inventory Coordination

Inventory information needs to remain organized and consistent across operational systems.

03

Shipping & Fulfillment

Shipping information and fulfillment tasks need to be tracked from order processing through completion.

WORKFLOW

From order to fulfillment.

01

Order Received

Review incoming customer orders and verify the information required for processing.

02

Order Processing

Confirm product information, quantities, customer details, and order status.

03

Inventory Check

Review inventory information and identify potential fulfillment issues.

04

Fulfillment & Shipping

Coordinate fulfillment and shipping information while keeping operational records updated.

05

Order Completed

Confirm completion and maintain organized records for operational visibility.
